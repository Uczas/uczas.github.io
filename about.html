My approach

From questions to useful solutions.

My work brings together mathematical modeling, optimization, and machine learning. I am interested in how these tools can explain complex systems, improve decisions, and support meaningful research.

As an educator, I help students develop a clearer understanding of mathematics and its applications. As a researcher, I explore problems in operations research and AI, including reinforcement learning and optimal control.

My writing explores education, emerging technology, and what it means to keep learning in a changing world.
